Why limits stop working

A timer that ends is an argument that starts.

The clock is the enemy

Time-based limits give nothing back for effort. Finishing homework early earns exactly the same as doing nothing.

You become enforcement

Every extension is a negotiation with you. The tool should carry that, not the parent.

They out-engineer you

Guest accounts, second browsers, reinstalls. Most tools are one search away from being defeated.

How it works

Set the exchange rate once.

  1. Agree what counts

    Homework, reading, chores, time outdoors, a walk. Set it together — agreement is what makes it survive week two.

  2. Lock what's being earned

    Games, social, video. Calls, messages, maps and the camera are never locked.

  3. They prove it, the phone opens

    A photo, a timer, step count. Verification is automatic, so you're not the referee at 9pm.

  4. You see progress, not their private life

    What was completed and what's unlocked. Not their chats, not their photos, not their browsing.

A line we don't cross

Not a monitoring app.

  • No message reading, no call logs, no browsing history, no location tracking.
  • Your child sees exactly what you see. Nothing is hidden from them.
  • Older teens can be given a mutual setup instead — you each hold a key, both sides locked.
  • Surveillance breaks trust and stops working the day they find it. Earned access keeps working because they agreed to it.

Straight answers

Questions worth asking first.

Can my child just uninstall it?

In the parent setup, no — removal needs your approval. That protection exists specifically for parent-and-child use and nothing else.

What about emergencies?

Calls, messages, maps and the camera are never blocked, in any configuration.

My teen will hate this.

Probably, if it's imposed. It's built to be agreed to — they set some of the tasks, they see the same screen you do, and nothing about them is hidden from them. If they won't agree to anything, a tool won't fix that.

How is this different from the free option on the phone?

Built-in controls hand out time. This hands out time in exchange for something done. If a time budget is working for your family, keep it — you don't need this.

Where's my data?

Task verification stays on the device wherever possible. We don't collect message content, location or browsing, so there's nothing of that kind to leak.
