Straight answers

Questions worth asking first.

Will it block YouTube? I need lectures.

Shorts get blocked, the rest of YouTube keeps working. Same for Instagram: Reels go, direct messages can stay if you want them.

What if there's a real emergency?

Calls, messages, maps, payments and your camera are never blocked. There's also an emergency override that works instantly — it just breaks the streak and, if you have a partner, tells them.

Can I not just uninstall it?

Yes, and we won't pretend otherwise — Google Play doesn't allow adult apps to prevent their own removal. What we do is make removal cost the streak, and tell your study partner. If you have no partner, the honest answer is that this app works on commitment, not force.

Does my photo go to a server?

Verification stays on your device wherever it can. Anything that needs checking is processed and discarded — no library of your desk is kept.

I'm not preparing for an exam.

It works the same for a thesis, a portfolio, a certification or a job hunt. The presets are a shortcut, not a requirement.
